Intern Abroad

Take on a paid international internship with real responsibility — from marketing to engineering — hosted by companies across AIESEC's global network.

Apply Now Program FAQs
Global Talent

What is Intern Abroad?

Global Talent connects you with paid internship roles at host companies abroad, spanning business, IT, engineering, marketing, and more — matched to your skills and career goals.

  • Duration: 6–78 weeks (role dependent)
  • Where: 120+ countries, across startups to multinational companies
  • Application fee: No application fee — an exchange participation fee applies once matched

Who it's for

University students and graduates seeking international, resume-building work experience in a professional field.

How it works

Your process, step by step

01

Explore & choose a project

Browse live opportunities by country, cause area, or date, and shortlist projects that fit your goals.

02

Apply online

Submit your application through the AIESEC global platform — no fee to apply.

03

Interview & match

Speak with the host committee, get matched to a project, and receive your acceptance.

04

Pre-departure preparation

Attend AIESEC in Bangladesh's pre-departure training covering logistics, safety, and cultural prep.

05

Fly & deliver impact

Arrive on-site, complete onboarding with your host, and get to work on real deliverables.

Good to know

Intern Abroad FAQs

Yes, Global Talent internships are paid by the host company, though pay varies by country and role.

Roles span business development, IT and software, marketing, finance, and engineering — you can filter by field when browsing.

Duration ranges from 6 weeks up to 78 weeks depending on the host company's role requirements.

Most roles are entry-level and open to students or recent graduates; some specialized roles may prefer prior coursework or project experience.

Ready to apply for Intern Abroad?

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis — the earlier you apply, the more project options you'll have.
