Youth leadership has had a home in Bangladesh since 1997.

AIESEC in Bangladesh is the national chapter of AIESEC — a global, non-political, independent, not-for-profit organization run entirely by young people.

Our story

From one committee to a national movement

AIESEC was founded globally in 1948 to build peace through youth leadership and cross-cultural exchange. AIESEC in Bangladesh joined the global network in 1997, opening exchange opportunities to Bangladeshi students for the first time.

Nearly three decades on, the organization has grown into four independently-run Local Committees based across Dhaka, sending students abroad on Volunteer, Talent, and Teacher exchanges — and bringing global talent into Bangladeshi organizations in return.

"AIESEC gave me my first taste of leading a team before I ever had a job title."

— Alumni Network, AIESEC in Bangladesh
Mission

Peace and the fulfilment of humankind's potential.

We provide young people in Bangladesh with leadership development experiences through international internship, volunteer, and teaching exchange programs, aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Vision

Every young Bangladeshi leading with a global mindset.

We want to be the leading platform for Bangladeshi youth to develop themselves as changemakers, at home and abroad, through practical cross-cultural experience.
